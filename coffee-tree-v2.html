04 · Important

Things to remember

Espresso is a method

Espresso is not a type of bean. It is a brewing method where water is pushed through finely ground coffee under pressure.

Milk drinks blend

Latte, cappuccino and flat white are not normally made as perfectly separated layers. Steamed milk and espresso combine, with microfoam integrated into the drink.

Americano ≠ Long Black

Both use espresso and water, but the preparation order differs. Long Black is traditionally water first, then espresso, helping preserve crema.

Macchiato has two meanings

Espresso Macchiato is espresso marked with a small amount of foam. Latte Macchiato is milk-dominant and the espresso marks the milk.

Iced percentages

When comparing iced drinks, beverage percentages should describe the liquid components and exclude the physical volume occupied by ice.

Recipes vary

Ratios shown here are conceptual reference points, not universal standards. A professional café should establish its own recipe cards.
